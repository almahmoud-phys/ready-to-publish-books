## Mandatory refresh triggers

Recheck live documentation when:

- starting a new edition or binding;
- changing trim, paper, ink, bleed, or page count;
- KDP Previewer disagrees with local calculations;
- answering a policy, disclosure, rights, or metadata question;
- the last verification is older than 90 days;
- KDP changes its calculator, upload UI, or available print options.

Record discrepancies instead of silently choosing whichever value is convenient. For production geometry, the newly downloaded template and KDP Previewer control.
