# Metadata and content compliance

Verified against official KDP help on 2026-08-15. Recheck policy immediately before title setup or republication.

## Exact matching

Cover and interior details must match KDP title setup, including title, subtitle, author/contributors, edition, and ISBN when shown. Treat spelling, punctuation, initials, and pen-name form as exact data, not cosmetic copy.

## Rights

The publisher is responsible for having publishing rights for all text, translations, fonts, cover images, illustrations, and other assets. Keep licenses, generation records, contributor agreements, and source provenance in the private production record.

## AI disclosure: two different surfaces

KDP currently asks publishers to inform Amazon about AI-generated text, images, or translations when publishing or republishing. It currently distinguishes:

- **AI-generated:** an AI tool created the actual text, image, or translation, even if substantially edited afterward.
- **AI-assisted:** a human created the content and used AI for editing, refinement, brainstorming, or error checking.

KDP currently says AI-assisted content does not require that upload-form disclosure.

This is a private KDP workflow disclosure. The cited KDP policy does **not** say that an AI notice must be printed in the book, cover, copyright page, description, or other reader-facing copy. Therefore:

- Remove reader-facing production disclosures when the publisher requests a conventional edition and no other law or contract requires them.
- Preserve private provenance and answer KDP's upload questions truthfully.
- Do not infer an upload-form answer merely from the absence of reader-facing text.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Pen names

Perform a practical exact-name catalog and web screen before locking a pen name. Record date, queries, result limitations, and owner approval. This reduces collision risk but is not trademark clearance or a guarantee of exclusivity.
