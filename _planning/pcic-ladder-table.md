## What this table implies for the book

Read against the manuscript: **A2 grammar appears in stories 01, 02, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09 and
10.** Story 01 alone contains rows 3, 9 and 10. So the choice is not "fix a few late stories" —
under a strict A1 reading the opening story is already A2, which is what makes the relabel option
(decision D1) the cheap one and the strict-A1 option expensive.

Rows 14, 16 and 17 stay banned. An A1–A2 book is not obliged to teach every A2 structure.
