## Purpose

Preserve this workspace as the repository's worked Stage-0 sample. It must not silently become
the first publication or donate sample evidence to another book.

## Authority order of records

- `constitution.md` governs intent and amendment boundaries.
- `manifest.yaml` owns stage-earned operational facts.
- `state.json` owns status and verdicts.
- `compliance_log.yaml` is append-only evidence.

Conflicts stop progression for owner reconciliation.

## Immutable-at-a-stage principles

- No manuscript work begins while the formal Stage-0 verdict is not GO.
- Sample evidence remains local to this workspace.
- Market numbers and authority claims are never invented or copied to unblock a gate.

## Course-correction triggers

- The sample is mistaken for a publication candidate.
- Machine-local evidence changes the clean-clone verdict.
- A pivot changes the reader problem or authority envelope.

## Amendment protocol

1. State the evidence and affected stages.
2. Obtain owner approval.
3. Append the change below.
4. Rerun every affected gate.

## Conflict rule

Any conflict among this file, `manifest.yaml`, `state.json`, and `compliance_log.yaml` stops work.
