Definitions, apply them strictly and identically to all six:
- adult_dignity: would an adult be embarrassed to be seen reading this on a train? 10 = not at

- local_closure: does THIS passage, read alone with no other passage, deliver a complete

- desire_to_continue: after finishing, do you want the next passage from this same book? 10 =
