# Gate C scoring notes — Spanish A2 Graded Reader, Volume 1

## Verdict

**LOOPBACK: Stage 2 (`chapter-writer`)**

**Book score: 3/10** — the floor dimension is **usefulness**. Gate C fails because multiple buyer-facing, learner-facing components contracted by the outline and manifest do not exist in the current book artifacts. Strong Spanish-story craft cannot compensate for their absence.

## What was judged

The completed Spanish story sequence, its outline, bible, summary chain, structural audit, niche contract, and the actual files in `chapters/` were evaluated. The existing independent Part I panel is useful evidence for Stories 01–04 but does not validate the full product, Story 05–10 quality, or unproduced components.

## Load-bearing evidence

1. **English parallel translations are absent.**
   - `manifest.yaml:L34-L44` identifies the interior as bilingual and budgets for English parallel text.
   - `outline/outline.md:L184-L190` requires English after each complete Spanish story; its placement is part of the pedagogical method.
   - The chapter directory contains only the ten Spanish story files and the excluded pipeline-proof file.
   - This directly breaks market, usefulness, coherence, and structure.

2. **Answer keys and learner-facing support are absent.**
   - `outline/outline.md:L204-L210` budgets for glossaries, comprehension questions **and answer keys**, plus introduction/how-to-use/back matter.
   - Each chapter presently ends with Spanish questions, for example `chapters/01-la-carta-sin-dueno.md:L87-L93`; no answer-key artifact exists.
   - The reader can neither check comprehension nor find the promised support outside the story text.

3. **The buyer-facing opening is absent.**
   - `outline/outline.md:L213-L217` requires the first 200 buyer-visible words to establish audience, Latin American Spanish, linked stories, and no-dictionary expectations without teacher voice.
   - The actual first manuscript content is Story 01, starting `chapters/01-la-carta-sin-dueno.md:L1-L3`; there is no frontmatter/opening artifact.

4. **The declared verification envelope is overbroad for current evidence.**
   - `research/charter.md:L25-L27` describes alignment, entailment/back-translation, and story-integrity checks.
   - `research/niche.md:L124-L126` records that alignment and back-translation are not built.
   - Until Stage 5 reconciles the claim, reader-facing copy must not inherit that envelope.

## Dimensions at or above floor

- **Originality, prose, voice:** The Spanish narrative contains real adult-oriented devices: silence, objects, work, and consequences rather than a child primer register. The existing blinded panel supports the Part I claim, especially Story 02.
- **Structural story spine:** Gate B passed after the annual-light repair. The Spanish narrative progression is coherent.

These passes do not close Gate C because the floor principle uses the weakest dimension.

## Required remediation — Stage 2

Create the missing contracted components without rewriting the Spanish story spine:

1. English translation after each complete Spanish story, with an auditable sentence/alignment plan.
2. Answer keys for every comprehension block; the keys must be traceable to the story text.
3. Learner-facing glossary presentation and a short how-to-use/opening section that fulfills the outline’s first-200-word contract without teacher voice.
4. Required back matter: honest next-step note and MIT wordlist attribution.
5. Do **not** make or repeat unverified alignment, back-translation, CEFR-certification, or comprehension-effectiveness claims. Either build the claimed checks at Stage 5 or narrow/remove the public claim.

After the missing components exist, return to Stage 3 only if the new material changes structural promises; otherwise re-run Stage 4 scoring with citations. Before packaging, Stage 5 must verify translation alignment and all reader-facing verification language.

## Uncertainty

No human readers or native-speaker reviewers are available by standing constraint. The Part I panel contains three independent model families but covers only Stories 01–04. This score therefore does not claim to settle the literary quality of Stories 05–10; it identifies the objectively missing deliverables that make the current artifact set unpublishable as the promised bilingual graded reader.
