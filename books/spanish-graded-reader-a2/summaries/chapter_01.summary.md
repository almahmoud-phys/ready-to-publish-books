# Story 01 — La carta sin dueño

## Contract and learning progression
- **Delivered reader capability:** Read a short present-tense Spanish narrative without stopping.
- **Grammar introduced:** Present indicative; `ser`, `estar`, `hay`; basic interrogatives; high-frequency irregular presents and third-person object clitics.
- **Vocabulary progression:** 16 new normalized types are closed in `bible/vocabulary-ledger.md`; later stories may treat them as known.

## Story state
Ana has worked the night kiosk in Puerto Lento for three months and knows only that an unnamed person held the job before her. After returning from the bathroom, she finds a newly placed letter addressed to Tomás Ferrer on the counter. Beto, who works at the boat shed, refuses to explain the name and twice says, “No hay nada.” Ana considers throwing the letter away but takes it home and places it under her bed with important things. Walking home, she sees a small silent yellow light far out on the water. The local decision is complete: a letter that arrived for someone else is now Ana’s responsibility.

## Exercise proof
- Five Spanish comprehension questions test the kiosk, the letter, Beto’s refusal, and Ana’s decision to keep the letter.

## Canon carried forward
- Puerto Lento, the pier kiosk, Ana’s three-month tenure, Beto, the letter for Tomás Ferrer, Beto’s “No hay nada,” and the distant light are established.
- Story 01 does **not** identify the prior kiosk worker as Tomás; Story 03 owns that revelation.
- **New canon candidates:** None.

## Setup
Ana will ask Rosa at the bakery about Tomás and must decide whether to reveal where the letter was found.
