# Story 07 — Está pasando algo

## Contract and learning progression
- **Delivered reader capability:** Follow simultaneous actions.
- **Grammar introduced:** Present progressive, simultaneous action, and `mientras` (a recorded house decision).
- **Vocabulary progression:** Eight new normalized types are closed; cumulative total is 76.

## Story state
Ana stays awake inside the kiosk to identify the person who leaves a letter. A noisy ordinary boat light on the water competes for her attention, but she chooses to watch the door. At four, the older woman who watched the bakery table enters carrying a new white letter. Ana recognizes her as Lucía. Lucía places the new letter beside the existing Tomás letter on the counter, in Tomás’s place; both women see each other but neither speaks. Ana neither takes the new letter nor asks a question, and Lucía leaves. Ana leaves both letters on the counter while the boat light continues moving outside.

## Exercise proof
- Five comprehension questions check Ana’s overnight purpose, what she can see, why she watches the door, who enters, and why she leaves the letters alone.
- “Una línea de tiempo en dos columnas” gives Ana’s three actions, three scrambled concurrent events, and blank second-column cells for the reader to complete.

## Canon carried forward
- Doña Lucía is the person who physically leaves the monthly kiosk letters.
- The letter route is direct placement at the counter; no carrier is involved.
- An ordinary passing boat light moves on the water on the same night; it is not Lucía’s annual lamp.
- Ana now knows who; the remaining question is why.
- **New canon candidates:** None.

## Setup
With the writer identified, Ana asks Rosa for the 1998 history behind Tomás and the letters.
