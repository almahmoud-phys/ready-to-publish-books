# Story 08 — Lo que pasó en 1998

## Contract and learning progression
- **Delivered reader capability:** Read a story told in the past.
- **Grammar introduced:** Regular preterite and past-time markers.
- **Vocabulary progression:** Fifteen new normalized types are closed; cumulative total is 91.

## Story state
At the kiosk, two letters remain on the counter and Ana asks Rosa directly what happened in 1998; she explains that she has seen Doña Lucía physically leave the new letter, the town’s silence is therefore already broken. Rosa pauses in the open doorway, holding her bread bag, and waits in silence before she comes to the counter and speaks. She says that during a storm a boat left the port, did not return, and Tomás Ferrer’s name was on its crew list. She did not see the men board, only read the list. Miguel later stored that list in the boat shed. Rosa states she has never sent or left a letter and asks Ana not to tell Lucía that she spoke. Ana promises not to tell and leaves the new letter on the kiosk counter.

## Exercise proof
- Five comprehension questions check the letter’s location, Rosa’s reluctance, Ana’s account of Lucía’s delivery, the boat’s fate, and Ana’s promise.
- The “Antes o ahora” table asks readers to sort eight statements between the 1998 past and the present scene.

## Canon carried forward
- A boat was lost in 1998 in bad weather and never found.
- Tomás’s name was on the crew list, but Rosa did not witness boarding.
- Rosa did not write or deliver letters and asks Ana to keep her confidence.
- **New canon candidates:** None.

## Setup
Ana goes to Lucía with the letter to test Rosa’s account of the crew list against Lucía’s account of her son.
