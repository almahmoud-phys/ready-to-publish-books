# Places — Volume 1

One town, never left. Rationale is in `outline/outline.md`: a single setting means the reader
re-meets the same nouns story after story, so vocabulary compounds instead of resetting.

**The town is invented.** It is not a real place and must never be presented as one — the charter
forbids claiming regional authenticity beyond "widely understood Latin American Spanish".

## Recurring nouns the reader is meant to over-learn

`el muelle · el mar · la luz · el barco · la noche · el pan · la playa · el agua · el bote · la carta`

These appear in at least four stories each, by design. If a draft replaces one with a synonym for
variety, that is a defect: variety here costs the reader the repetition the method depends on.
