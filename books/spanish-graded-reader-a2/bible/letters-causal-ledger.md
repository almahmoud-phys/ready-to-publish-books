# The letters — causal ledger

Written after the round-2 Gate 1 review, which found the letter engine physically incoherent:
story 01 finds a letter *inside the kiosk*, story 06 says letters have arrived for years, story 10
reveals Doña Lucía puts letters *into the sea* — and nothing explained how a sea-posted letter
reaches a kiosk. "Unresolved grief may remain emotionally unresolved; the physical mechanism may
not remain incoherent." Correct. This file fixes it, and every story contract must obey it.

## The mechanism

**Tomás Ferrer worked the pier kiosk before Ana did.** That single fact carries the whole engine.

| Element | Fact |
|---|---|
| Writer | Doña Lucía Ferrer, Tomás's mother |
| Recipient | Tomás Ferrer, her son, who did not board the boat that was lost in 1998 |
| Deposit point A | **The kiosk** — his old post. She leaves a letter there, monthly, before dawn. |
| Deposit point B | **The sea** — once a year, on the anniversary, she rows out with a lamp and puts one letter in the water. That lamp is the light. |
| Carrier | Nobody. There is no delivery system. She walks fifty metres and puts it on the counter. |
| Discovery point | Ana, because Ana now holds Tomás's job and opens the kiosk. |
| Purpose | Not belief that he will read them. A place to put the sentences. |

## What this fixes

- **How a letter gets into the kiosk:** it was never posted anywhere. She places it by hand, at
  the counter where her son used to stand. Ordinary, physical, and sad.
- **Why letters have come "for years":** monthly, since 1998. Ana is simply the first kiosk worker
  who did not already know to leave them alone.
- **Why Ana is the one who finds them:** she inherited the post. Story 01 must establish that she
  is new to it — three months, not three years.
- **Why the stranger in story 03 knows her name:** everyone knows who took Tomás's job. It is the
  smallest town on the coast. Nothing supernatural, and no twist that later deflates.
- **Why the light is annual, not monthly:** the review is right that a monthly ritual seen
  repeatedly would flatten. Ana sees Lucía's annual lamp in story 01 because story 01 falls near
  the anniversary. Stories 01–09 take place over roughly five weeks. Story 10 returns almost one
  year after story 01, on the next anniversary; it is the second and only other annual lamp event.
  The light in story 07 is an ordinary boat light, not Lucía's lamp.

## What is deliberately NOT explained

Why Doña Lucía writes to a son who did not board. Rosa believes he was lost with the boat; Doña
Lucía knows he was not, and has never corrected anyone. Where Tomás actually went is **not
answered in volume 1** and must not be teased as though it will be. That is the volume-2 hook, and
the closing note says so plainly rather than baiting the reader.

## Beto's reason to stay (was previously unmotivated)

Beto wants to leave because he cannot stand a town that answers every question with *no hay nada*.
That is a causal reason, not a mood. When Ana finally says out loud that there *is* something —
and the town does not fall apart — the thing he was leaving is gone. He stays.

This is why the phrase must NOT appear in story 10. Its absence is the ending.
